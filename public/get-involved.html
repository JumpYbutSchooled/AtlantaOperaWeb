Give

Gifts to The Atlanta Opera are tax-deductible and support its productions and the education programs that reach students across Georgia.

Giving circles

Annual gifts come with thank-you benefits that grow with each level.

To give, email the Development team or call the office and ask for Development.

  • Friends Circle, from $125: your name in the program, and from $500, final dress rehearsal tickets
  • Patrons Circle, from $2,500: lounge passes at every opera, opening night parties and backstage tours
  • Directors Circle, $25,000 and up: rehearsal invitations, first pick of season seats, and trips with the General Director

Email to give 404-881-8801

Other ways to give

Mail a check to The Atlanta Opera, Attn: Development, 1575 Northside Drive NW, Building 300, Suite 350, Atlanta, GA 30318. You can also give stock, or ask your employer to match your gift.

Giving stock (PDF) development@atlantaopera.org

Gifts in your will

Leave a gift through a will or trust, an IRA or life insurance, or a donor-advised fund. Everyone who does joins the Barbara D. Stewart Legacy Society, named for a former board president whose own gift helped the company through the recession.

The L’amour Gala

An evening of opera, dinner and auctions at Flourish Atlanta, raising money for the company. This year’s gala honors Dr. Harold “Hal” Brody and Don Smith, longtime supporters of the arts in Atlanta. The co-chairs are Susan Anderson, Caroline Hardin and Alfredo Martin.

Tables and sponsorships start at $12,500.

For tickets, tables and sponsorships, contact Kristin Haynes, Institutional Giving Manager, at 470-427-2236 or development@atlantaopera.org.

Volunteer and be on stage

Volunteer

Help out behind the scenes. Volunteers can:

  • Sell merchandise at Shop Opera
  • Stand on stage while the lights are set (“light walking”)
  • Help at final dress rehearsals and special events
  • Cover the front desk, filing and the archives
  • Students can earn course credit

Email to volunteer

Supernumeraries

“Supers” are the opera’s extras: people on stage who don’t sing or speak. Which roles a production needs varies from show to show.

None are needed right now, but the company keeps names on file all year and gets in touch when a role comes up.

Email to be kept on file

Open as of October 2026. Put the job title in the subject line, and no phone calls, please.

Artistic Operations Manager

Full-time. Runs the schedules for productions, rehearsals and the Studio Artists, and the company’s planning software. $60,000 to $65,000, plus benefits.

Costume Director
