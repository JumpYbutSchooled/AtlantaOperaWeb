Before you go

What to wear

There’s no dress code. You’ll see everything from jeans to evening gowns, and plenty of people dress up for fun.

Getting there

Doors open 90 minutes before the show. Operas start on time, and anyone arriving late is seated at a break, so leave extra time for Atlanta traffic.

Free pre-show talk

An hour before each mainstage performance there’s a free talk about the opera and the people who wrote it.

Following along

English supertitles are shown above the stage, so you can follow operas sung in other languages. Reading the story beforehand helps too.

Food and drink

Drinks are allowed in the theater, except in the front rows by the orchestra pit, where it’s bottled water only.

Questions

The ticket office is open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm.

Coming in 2029

The complete Ring

Since 2023 the company has been building Wagner’s four-opera Ring cycle, about seventeen hours of music. In June 2029 it will be performed whole, twice.
