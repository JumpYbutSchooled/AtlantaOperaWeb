The story

In 1871 a small group of students set out from the newly founded Fisk University in Nashville to save their school from going under. They toured the country and then Europe, singing spirituals born in slavery to audiences who had never heard them on a concert stage.

Their concerts paid for Jubilee Hall, kept Fisk open, and carried this music around the world. Jubilee tells their story in song and narration, performed with voices only.

Songs include “Swing Low, Sweet Chariot,” “Go Down, Moses,” “Sometimes I Feel Like a Motherless Child,” “Let Us Break Bread Together,” “Do Lord Remember Me,” “All Them Bones” and “I Believe I’ll Go Back Home.”
