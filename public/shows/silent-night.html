Silent Night

Music by Kevin Puts, words by Mark Campbell

November 7–15, 2026 · Cobb Energy Performing Arts Centre

Buy tickets Dates and times

The story

Christmas Eve, 1914, on the Western Front in Belgium. Scottish, French and German soldiers dug in a few hundred yards apart have just lost friends in a bloody, pointless attack.

That night a German tenor, sent to sing for the Crown Prince, comes back to the trenches and sings across no-man’s land. The Scots answer with bagpipes, the three lieutenants agree to stop for the night, and the men climb out to swap drinks and photos, hold a Christmas mass and bury their dead together.

When their commanders hear about it, every unit is punished and sent away. The opera is based on the French film Joyeux Noël.

  • Won the 2012 Pulitzer Prize for Music
  • First performed by Minnesota Opera on November 12, 2011
  • Directed by Tomer Zvulun and conducted by Iván López Reynoso
  • This production began at Wexford Festival Opera in Ireland, where it won Best Opera at the Irish Times Theatre Awards
  • Shared with Florida Grand Opera and Opera Carolina

Who wrote it

Kevin Puts

Composer

An American composer who won the Pulitzer Prize for this opera and has written for the Metropolitan Opera, the Philadelphia Orchestra and Renée Fleming.

Mark Campbell

Librettist

Has written the words for some 40 operas, including The (R)evolution of Steve Jobs, which won a Grammy, and As One.

Content advisory
