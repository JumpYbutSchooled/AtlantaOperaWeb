All Is Calm: The Christmas Truce of 1914

Written by Peter Rothstein, vocal arrangements by Erick Lichte and Timothy C. Takach

December 2–27, 2026 · Theatrical Outfit

Buy tickets Dates and times

The story

The same Christmas truce, told a different way. There are no invented characters: a group of ten men speak the real words of soldiers who were there, taken from their letters, diaries and official records.

Between the words they sing the songs those soldiers sang, from patriotic tunes and trench songs to the carols that drifted across no-man’s land that night. It runs about an hour and twenty minutes, in an intimate theater.

  • First heard on Minnesota Public Radio on December 21, 2007
  • Made together with Theatrical Outfit, where it’s performed
