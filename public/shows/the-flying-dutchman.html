The Flying Dutchman

Music and words by Richard Wagner

March 13–21, 2027 · Cobb Energy Performing Arts Centre

Buy tickets Dates and times

The story

A sea captain who once cursed heaven is doomed to sail forever. Every seven years he may come ashore to look for a woman who will be faithful to him until death, the only thing that can set him free.

Caught in a storm off Norway, he meets the captain Daland and offers him a fortune for a night’s shelter and his daughter’s hand. Daland’s daughter Senta has been obsessed with the legend of the Flying Dutchman for years, but she has already made promises to the hunter Erik.

This production tells the old ghost story with a modern eye, with Grammy winner Ryan Speedo Green as the Dutchman.

  • First performed in Dresden on January 2, 1843
  • A Tomer Zvulun production, conducted by Iván López Reynoso
  • Wagner wrote both the music and the words
