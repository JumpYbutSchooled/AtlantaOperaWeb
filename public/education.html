Studio Tour: Cinderella, The Remix Opera

Rossini’s La Cenerentola retold in English and Spanish for young audiences. Ella learns that being yourself and being kind are the greatest strengths of all.

  • Grades K–12
  • 45 minutes, then a 5-minute Q&A with the singers
  • $800 a performance, $100 off each when you book two or more
  • Title I scholarships and discounts available

Storytime

Teaching artists sing their way through a picture book, building early reading skills like vocabulary and following a story. Choose from Jojo Finds His Voice, Opera Cat or Diva Dolores and the Opera House Mouse.

New this year: a bilingual English and Spanish Storytime with Moon Rope, and adaptive Storytimes for children under 5 and for special education classrooms.

  • Pre-K to 1st grade
  • 30 minutes, starting as early as 8:30 am
  • $250 a performance, up to 100 children each
  • Title I scholarships and discounts available
  • All year

Workshops and classes

Workshops

In person or online, taught by teaching artists and staff. Topics include an introduction to opera, voice types, opera history, careers in the arts, lighting and props, and page to stage.

Masterclasses

Professional singers work with students on their songs, musicianship, diction and interpretation, alone or in groups.

Filmed versions of the touring operas, free for every school and available all year. Each is 40 to 45 minutes and comes with a teacher’s guide for grades K–2, 3–5 and 9–12. Cinderella: The Remix Opera joins them in February 2027.
